各位親愛的朋友，您現在正處於一份失落的情緒中，讓我覺得悲傷難過。
My dear friends, you are now experiencing a feeling of loss, which makes me feel sad and sorrowful.
我們的身體還未出生前早已跟天地同在。
Our bodies existed with heaven and earth even before we were born.
死亡後也將與天地同在。
And after death, they will also be one with heaven and earth.
當我們的親友往生後，悲傷之情在所難免。
When our loved ones pass away, sadness is an inevitable feeling.
但是收拾失親之痛後，可以將思念之情，轉化為感念！
But after processing the pain of loss, we can transform our longing into gratitude！
同時也感恩這天地之間的所有一切。
And at the same time, give thanks for all that exists between heaven and earth.
並且祝福他們，能夠順利地到達最適合他們自己的地方！
And bless them, so they may smoothly reach the place most suitable for them！
而得到內心的安穩與平靜。
Finding inner security and peace.
就在這宇宙天地之間。
Right here, within this universe, between heaven and earth.
我們也可以祈禱，祈願早已超越生死世間的佛陀及諸大菩薩的慈悲接引！
We can also pray, wishing for the compassionate guidance of the Buddhas and Bodhisattvas, who have already transcended the realms of life and death！
圓滿其修行的志業。
To perfectly fulfill their spiritual path.
同時祈願自己跟其他人，在這一生中也能修行圓滿。
And at the same time, we pray that we ourselves and others, may also achieve complete spiritual fulfillment in this lifetime.
在此感恩您！
Here, I thank you！
祈願您一切幸福與圓滿！
And wish you all happiness and completeness!
