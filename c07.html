Speaker 1: 各位親愛的朋友們，歡迎您來到這趟心靈之旅。請允許我以溫柔、平穩的語調，引導您深入內在的世界。 Speaker 2: My dear friends, welcome to this journey of the mind. Please allow me, with a gentle and steady tone, to guide you into your inner world. Speaker 1: 如果我們觀照自我身心的現況，體驗生命存在的過程。 Speaker 2: If we observe the current state of our mind and body, and experience the process of life's existence. Speaker 1: 會有與自己在一起的喜悅！ 知道身心及一切現象，如電光、水泡，如暫時的聚落，如幻化的魔術。 Speaker 2: There will be a joy of simply being with oneself! Knowing that body, mind, and all phenomena are like flashes of lightning, bubbles in water, temporary settlements, or illusions created by magic. Speaker 1: 那就找不到真實的實體，認為這個人這個聲音這個形態和名字、思想，那就是我，那是我的，它是我的代表。 Speaker 2: Then one cannot find a true entity, believing that this person, this voice, this form and name, these thoughts, are 'me,' 'mine,' or 'my representative.' Speaker 1: 您會發現一切皆是虛妄，它只是內心執著有為法的往前推進中因緣碰觸到的短暫現象。 Speaker 2: You will find that everything is illusory, merely a temporary phenomenon encountered by conditions as the mind moves forward, clinging to conditioned existence. Speaker 1: 就像正在觀看一場戲劇的人，您只是靜默，優雅的欣賞。您只會如實的陳述，學習到了什麼？看到了什麼？ Speaker 2: Just like someone watching a play, you simply observe in silence, appreciating with grace. You will only truthfully state: what have you learned? What have you seen? Speaker 1: 您的語言是柔軟的、清楚的、如實的、讚嘆的。 Speaker 2: Your language will be gentle, clear, truthful, and appreciative. Speaker 1: 那就不可能與人吵架、打誑語、挑撥離間、講迷惑人的言語。 Speaker 2: Then it becomes impossible to quarrel with others, speak falsely, sow discord, or utter confusing words. Speaker 1: 這能減少因為言語可能的，帶來生活上的關係緊張。 Speaker 2: This can reduce the potential for relationship tension in life caused by words. Speaker 1: 在此感恩您，祈願您一切,幸福與圓滿！ Speaker 2: Hereby, I express my deepest gratitude to you, wishing you all happiness and completeness!
