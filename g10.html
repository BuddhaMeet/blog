"Alright, please relax your body and mind. Follow my guidance, and let your breath lead you into a space of tranquility. Now, gently close your eyes and feel the stillness between every inhale and exhale."
此刻，您的心中或許浮現了極為強烈的情緒，甚至想著... ...我要殺了他！
At this moment, perhaps extremely intense emotions have arisen within your heart,even thoughts like... ...I want to kill him!
請輕柔地覺察，這是個多麼沉重的想法。
Gently acknowledge,what a heavy thought this is.
您可以想像得到，它或許是源於深層的猜疑、忌妒，甚至積壓已久的憎恨。
You can imagine,it might stem from deep-seated suspicion,jealousy, or even long-held hatred.
人類的生命，是如此的脆弱與寶貴，要傷害一個人，在行動上或許看似容易。
Human life is so fragile and precious;to harm someone might seem easy in action.

Yet,please consider,if you act in this way, wouldn't you lose someone who might be a worthy opponent?
那將會是多麼的寂寞與空虛啊！
How lonely and empty that would be!
更何況，這位時常讓您感到惱怒，甚至造成您某些損害的人...
What's more,this person who often irritates you,and even causes you some harm...
...他其實也可能是一位獨特的「逆增上緣」善知識，透過考驗，不斷提醒您，要放下對這短暫身體的執著，以及對虛妄名位的追逐呢！
...he might actually be a unique 'adversarial benefactor,' who, through challenges, continuously reminds you to let go of attachment to this decaying body, and the pursuit of illusory fame and status!
如果失去了這樣的機緣，豈不是一種莫大的可惜？
If such an opportunity were lost, wouldn't it be a great pity?

Please consider further,if,in the grip of great anger,you were to commit an irreparable mistake...

...wouldn't this allow that malicious adversary to feel a triumphant ecstasy, even in the unseen realms?

Now, please broaden your heart. If you can, with a compassionate heart, pity this foolish person controlled by anger and affliction...
...原諒他的無禮與過失，並且不為所動，保持內心的平靜與穩定。
...forgive his disrespect and transgressions, and remain unswayed, maintaining inner peace and stability.

Then,the unfortunate one will continue to carry his own burning anguish,worries,and resentment,sinking into suffering,while your heart can remain clear and tranquil.
這，才是真正的智者所為。
This,is truly the action of a wise one.

Here,I am deeply grateful for your listening and understanding.

May you be well in all things, nd may your heart be filled with happiness and completeness.
