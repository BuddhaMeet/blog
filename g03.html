好的，親愛的練習者，請您輕輕閉上雙眼，感受此刻的寧靜。
Alright, dear practitioner, gently close your eyes and feel the stillness of this moment.
讓我們專注於心中的感受，關於憎恨，特別是針對那些我們曾經討厭的師長。
Let us focus on the feelings within our hearts, concerning hatred, especially towards those teachers we once disliked.
無論是您喜歡或不喜歡的人，當他們有所成就，這正好就是我們學習的絕佳機會。
Whether it's someone you like or dislike, when they achieve something, it presents a perfect opportunity for us to learn.
如果沒有比較，我們就不會知道自己有所不足；而知曉不足，正是激發我們奮發圖強的內在動力。
If there's no comparison, we won't realize our shortcomings; and knowing our shortcomings is precisely the inner drive that inspires us to strive for improvement.
所謂「活到老學到老」，能夠持續地學習，是人生中一件非常快樂且有意義的事。
As the saying goes, "Live and learn," the ability to continue learning is a truly joyful and meaningful aspect of life.
或許對於這樣的老師，您曾經有著百般的不滿與抱怨。
Perhaps regarding such a teacher, you once held countless dissatisfactions and complaints.
然而，如果您能夠化解心中的偏見，包容他們的缺點，並且努力從中學習以改善自己，那麼您必定能夠獲得更高的成就與成長。
However, if you can resolve the prejudices within your heart, embrace their flaws, and diligently learn from them to improve yourself, then you will undoubtedly achieve greater success and growth.
或許有一天，當您反過來回顧自己的成功歷程時，您就會恍然大悟。
Perhaps one day, when you look back at your own journey of success, you will realize something profound.
您會明白，原來所有有所成就的人，包括您自己本身在內，其實都依舊留存著許多惡習和不圓滿之處；但更重要的是，這些都是可以被理解與容許的。
You will understand that all accomplished individuals, including yourself, still retain many imperfections and habits that are not ideal; but more importantly, these are acceptable and understandable.
因為人生本來就是在不圓滿中，透過不斷地學習與體驗，我們才能得以真正的增長與茁壯。
For life itself is a continuous process of learning and experience within imperfection, and it is through this that we truly grow and thrive.
在此，我感恩您的聆聽，並誠摯地祈願您的一切，都能夠充滿幸福與圓滿！
Here, I offer my gratitude for your listening, and I sincerely wish that all aspects of your life may be filled with happiness and fulfillment!
