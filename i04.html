Speaker 1: 各位朋友，今天我們將探索一個關於內心滿足與平衡的主題。 Speaker 2: Dear friends, today we will explore a topic concerning inner contentment and balance. Speaker 1: 關於「攀緣多求」。 Speaker 2: Regarding "attachment and excessive desire." Speaker 1: 佛陀曾教導我們，多欲之人，因過度追求利益，會遭受許多苦惱。 Speaker 2: The Buddha once taught us that those with many desires, due to their excessive pursuit of gain, will experience much suffering. Speaker 1: 相反地，少欲之人，無所求亦無所欲，便不會有這些憂患。 Speaker 2: Conversely, those with few desires, having no seeking and no craving, will be free from such troubles. Speaker 1: 即使是天性少欲之人，也應當持續修習這份心境。 Speaker 2: Even those naturally possessing few desires should continue to cultivate this state of mind. Speaker 1: 更何況，少欲能夠生出種種功德。 Speaker 2: Furthermore, having few desires can generate various virtues. Speaker 1: 少欲之人，心境清明，不會諂媚他人以求迎合。 Speaker 2: Those with few desires possess a clear mind and will not flatter others to seek their approval. Speaker 1: 他們也不會被外在的感官所牽引。 Speaker 2: Nor will they be swayed by external sensory perceptions. Speaker 1: 奉行少欲者，心境自然坦然，無所憂慮或畏懼。 Speaker 2: Those who practice few desires will have a naturally tranquil heart, free from worry or fear. Speaker 1: 處事圓滿，常常感到富足，從不覺得有所不足。 Speaker 2: They handle matters with completeness, often feel abundant, and never experience insufficiency. Speaker 1: 擁有少欲之心，便能達到內心的涅槃。 Speaker 2: Possessing a mind with few desires leads to inner Nirvana. Speaker 1: 這就是少欲的真諦。 Speaker 2: This is the true meaning of having few desires. Speaker 1: 以上這段話是佛陀的教誨，它其實非常的淺顯易懂。 Speaker 2: The passage above is a teaching from the Buddha, and it is actually very simple and easy to understand. Speaker 1: 其中所提到的「諸根」，是指我們的眼、耳、鼻、舌、身、以及意根。 Speaker 2: The term "諸根" (all roots) mentioned refers to our eyes, ears, nose, tongue, body, and mind roots. Speaker 1: 而對於我們凡夫俗子來說，「涅槃」可以被比喻為一種內心的平靜。 Speaker 2: And for us ordinary mortals, "Nirvana" can be likened to a state of inner peace. Speaker 1: 其實，能夠滿足於現況的人，他的內心自然會踏實而明朗。 Speaker 2: In truth, those who can be content with their current situation naturally possess a steadfast and clear inner heart. Speaker 1: 這與多求之人的欲望形成對比，他們的欲望往往投射在不可知的未來。 Speaker 2: This contrasts with the desires of those who seek much,whose cravings are often projected into an unknown future. Speaker 1: 因此，少欲知足之人，確實能比一般人感受到更多的幸福與快樂。 Speaker 2: Therefore, individuals with few desires and contentment can indeed experience more happiness and joy than others. Speaker 1: 讓我們用一顆平等心，去溫柔地看待世間的一切吧。 Speaker 2: Let us gently view all things in the world with an impartial heart. Speaker 1: 這，才是真正平衡而美好的人生。 Speaker 2: This,indeed,is the truly balanced and beautiful life. Speaker 1: 在此，我深深地感恩您的聆聽。 Speaker 2: Here,I extend my deepest gratitude for your listening. Speaker 1: 祈願您一切順遂，充滿幸福與圓滿！ Speaker 2: May all be well with you,filled with happiness and completeness!
