親愛的朋友，請放鬆你的身心。
Dear friend, please relax your body and mind.
我們的成長，必定要依賴老師、親友、同事等，社會上許多人的幫助，才能夠成就。
Our growth,unquestionably relies on the help of many people in society, such as teachers,relatives, and colleagues,in order to be truly achieved.
如果我們當初，不信任他們，對他們傲慢、無禮，那麼就不可能有身心健全的成長。
If we, back then,did not trust them,were arrogant or disrespectful towards them,then a healthy growth of mind and body would have been impossible.
然而，一旦我們有了財富、名位之後，卻開始擔心他們的所作所為，會造成你的損失。
However,once we acquire wealth and status,we begin to worry that their actions might cause us loss.
而對他們的能力有所猜疑，這是相當矛盾的。
And to harbor suspicion about their capabilities– this is quite contradictory.
由於明了一切世間，皆是虛幻、變化，並無實體。
Understanding that everything in this world is illusory,changing, and without inherent substance.
菩薩只有真誠的分享，適度的協助你成長，並不會擔心自己有所損失。
odhisattvas offer only sincere sharing, and provide moderate assistance for your growth, never fearing their own loss.
因此在菩薩的國度裡，只有和樂、自在、 平等、互助的伴侶，並無互相爭奪的世界。
Therefore,in the realm of Bodhisattvas,there are only companions of harmony and freedom,equality and mutual aid,and no world of mutual contention.
如果我們的財富，不是為了分享，讓彼此內心成長， 那是為了什麼？
If our wealth,is not for sharing,to foster our inner growth,then what is it for?
難道是為了私慾，讓自己成為慾望的奴隸嗎？當然不是！
Is it to serve selfish desires,to make ourselves slaves to craving?Certainly not!
所以，信任他人，分享榮耀，才是我們要做的。
Therefore,trusting others,and sharing glory,is what we are meant to do.
只要適當的瞭解與防範， 何須猜疑？
With appropriate understanding and prevention,why the need for suspicion?
在此感恩您，祈願您一切，幸福與圓滿！
With this,I thank you,and I wish you all happiness and fulfillment!
願您內心平靜，活出光芒。Namaste.
May your heart be peaceful,and may you shine brightly. Namaste.
