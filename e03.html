Speaker 1: 親愛的朋友，歡迎來到這片寧靜的空間。 讓我們一同探索內心的智慧。 Speaker 2: Dear friend, welcome to this tranquil space. Let us explore the wisdom within ourselves together. Speaker 1:享樂與排斥。 Speaker 2:Pleasure and Rejection. Speaker 1:當我們迷戀在一個地方，享樂其中，常常達到忘我的地步時。 Speaker 2:When we are captivated by a place, immersed in its enjoyment, often reaching a state of self-forgetfulness. Speaker 1:一旦接受到外力干擾的時候，第一個反應一定是，極力的反抗與排斥，想要回到享樂狀態。 Speaker 2:The moment we encounter external interference, our first reaction will undoubtedly be strong resistance and rejection, wanting to return to that state of enjoyment. Speaker 1:在這裡我們可以很清楚的看出，享樂與排斥兩者不能分開。 Speaker 2:Here, we can clearly see that pleasure and rejection cannot be separated. Speaker 1:然而人的生活的全部，包括許多面向，享樂只是其中之一。 Speaker 2:However, the entirety of human life encompasses many aspects, and pleasure is just one of them. Speaker 1:如果只顧著享樂，生活其他部份完全不打理，那麼將會造成許多方面的不平衡，與及他人的困擾。 Speaker 2:If we only focus on pleasure, completely neglecting other parts of life, it will lead to imbalances in many areas, as well as troubles for others. Speaker 1:嚴重的話，還會影響到健康。 Speaker 2:In severe cases, it can even affect our health. Speaker 1:禪樂也是一樣，不只是打坐時候，其他時間和地點，都俱備平衡之美。 Speaker 2:Zen joy is the same; it's not just during meditation, but at all other times and places, it embodies the beauty of balance. Speaker 1:只要用心接納，處處都能有，禪悅、法喜的。 Speaker 2:As long as we accept with our hearts, Zen delight and Dharma bliss can be found everywhere. Speaker 1:在此感恩您，祈願您一切，幸福與圓滿！ Speaker 2:Here, I thank you, and I wish you all happiness and completeness!
