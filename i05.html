Speaker 1: 親愛的朋友，請放鬆您的身心，讓我們一同踏上一段內在的旅程。 Speaker 2: Dear friend, please relax your body and mind, and let us embark on an inner journey together. Speaker 1: 今天，我們將靜心思考一個主題：關於「作發財夢」的深層意涵。 Speaker 2: Today, we will mindfully contemplate a theme: the profound meaning behind "making a fortune dream." Speaker 1: 在古老的《佛遺教經》中，佛陀慈悲地教誨我們： Speaker 2: In the ancient Sutra of Bequeathed Teachings, the Buddha compassionately taught us: Speaker 1: 「汝等比丘。若欲脫諸苦惱。當觀知足。」 Speaker 2: "Oh bhikshus, if you wish to be free from all suffering and distress, you must contemplate and practice contentment." Speaker 1: 「知足之法。即是富樂安穩之處。」 Speaker 2: "The principle of contentment is indeed the dwelling place of wealth, joy, and peace." Speaker 1: 「知足之人。雖臥地上。猶為安樂。」 Speaker 2: "A contented person, even if lying on the ground, still feels at peace." Speaker 1: 「不知足者。雖處天堂。亦不稱意。」 Speaker 2: "One who is discontent, even if residing in heaven, will not find satisfaction." Speaker 1: 「不知足者。雖富而貧。」 Speaker 2: "The discontented, though wealthy, are truly poor." Speaker 1: 「知足之人。雖貧而富。」 Speaker 2: "The contented, though poor, are truly wealthy." Speaker 1: 「不知足者。常為五欲所牽。為知足者之所憐愍。是名知足。」 Speaker 2: "The discontented are constantly swayed by the five desires, and are even pitied by those who are content. This, is the true meaning of contentment." Speaker 1: 在此，我深深地感恩您的聆聽與存在，並衷心祈願您的一切都充滿幸福與圓滿！ Speaker 2: Here, I deeply thank you for your listening and presence, and sincerely wish that all your endeavors are filled with happiness and completeness!
