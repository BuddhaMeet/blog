親愛的朋友，歡迎來到今天的冥想與心靈指引。
Dear friend, welcome to today's meditation and spiritual guidance.
我們今天的反思主題是：懈怠。
Our reflection for today is on: Indolence.
無所事事，虛度光陰。
Doing nothing, wasting precious time.
我們如果整天無所事事，生活沒有重心，
If we spend our entire day doing nothing, living without a sense of purpose,
這樣不但虛度了光陰， 也會讓自己的精神委靡不振。
Not only do we waste precious time, but our spirit will also become listless and disheartened.
我們應該想想 佛 、菩薩的精神，
We should reflect on the spirit of the Buddhas and Bodhisattvas,
他們生生世世 ，不吝惜生命、 財產，
Throughout countless lifetimes, they never hesitated to offer their lives and possessions,
忍辱 、布施，
Enduring insults and practicing generosity,
只為了培養我們凡夫的 一點點善根。
All just to cultivate even a tiny seed of goodness within us ordinary beings.
在 佛 、菩薩面前，看看自己的渺小和脆弱，
In the presence of the Buddhas and Bodhi-sattvas, observe your own insignificance and vulnerability,
生起慚愧之心，
Let a sense of shame arise,
激勵自己不斷努力， 不斷地發願。
Inspiring you to strive ceaselessly and make continuous vows.
砥礪自己學習 佛、 菩薩， 慈悲偉大的精神，
Encourage yourself to learn from the compassionate and magnificent spirit of the Buddhas and Bodhi-sattvas,
努力付出自己的心力， 為社會、人群服務，
Diligently dedicate your efforts to serving society and all beings,
開展自己的生命，這才是踏實的人生。
Unfold your own life; this is truly a grounded and fulfilling existence.
在此感恩您，祈願您一切幸福與圓滿！
Here, we express our gratitude to you, wishing you all happiness and completeness!
